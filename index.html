About Iron Pulse Fitness

Since opening in Baner, Iron Pulse Fitness has grown into one of Pune's most trusted training spaces. We keep our floor simple: quality equipment, coaches who actually watch your form, and a schedule that works whether you train at 6 AM or 9 PM. No gimmicks — just a gym built around consistent, honest progress.

Modern Equipment

Full range of free weights, machines and functional gear, well maintained and always available.

Certified Trainers

Every coach on our floor is certified and trained to correct form before it becomes a habit.

Clean & Safe Environment

Sanitised equipment, proper ventilation and a layout that gives every member room to train.

Personalized Training

Programs adjusted to your goals, schedule and current fitness level — not a one-size plan.
